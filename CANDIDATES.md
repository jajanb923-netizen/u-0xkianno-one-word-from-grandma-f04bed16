# Candidates — One Word From Grandma

Judged against the judge's five criteria, in order: (1) reads as the single
word a grandma would text about AI; (2) warm, a little baffled grandma
voice; (3) funny in one word with no caption; (4) tied to AI, robots or
machines; (5) fresh, instantly readable. Tie-breaks also favour fewer bytes.

| # | word | verdict |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | robit | **WINNER.** The typo she would actually type for "robot" — the typo IS her voice (1, 2). Funny the instant you read it (3). Robot-tied, unmistakably (4). Fresher than plain "robot", still instantly readable (5). 5 bytes — the shortest candidate, which also wins exact ties. |
| 2 | doohickey | Runner-up. Grandma's word for a gadget she cannot name: warm, baffled, funny. But it fits any gadget, so it is weaker on (4), and at 9 bytes it loses a tie to robit. |
